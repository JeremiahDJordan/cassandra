* Strings, `boolean`, `uuid` and frozen collections: `!=`, `NOT IN` and the range operators `<`, `>`,
`<=`, `>=`
* Frozen collections: `IN` of several values, or `IN ?` with a list bound to the marker
* Frozen tuples and user-defined types: the range operators

These are evaluated with `ALLOW FILTERING`, as on columns without an index. `LIKE` needs a supporting index, also with
`ALLOW FILTERING`: a string index without an `index_analyzer` or `query_analyzer`.
