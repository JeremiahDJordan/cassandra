* Strings, `boolean`, `uuid`, `blob` and frozen collections: `!=`, `NOT IN` and the range operators `<`, `>`,
`<=`, `>=`
* Frozen collections: `IN` of several values
* Frozen tuples and user-defined types: the range operators
* Strings with an `index_analyzer`: `LIKE`

These are evaluated with `ALLOW FILTERING`, as on columns without an index.
